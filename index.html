Quick strategy guide

Practical exploitative principles for the player pool you described.

1. Isolate limpers

Raise larger for value when weak players limp and call too much. Be more selective with marginal hands when several players are likely to call.

2. Size up with strong value hands

Against players who call too wide, top pair with a strong kicker, two pair, sets and strong made hands can often support larger value bets. Board texture and ranges still matter.

3. Bluff less, but not never

Target players and runouts that actually create folds. Semi-bluffs with equity are usually preferable to trying to push a calling station off a pair.

4. Treat raises as information, not proof

A passive player raising big on the river is often value-heavy, but make the decision from evidence—not a stereotype alone.

5. Keep a baseline

GTO is a useful reference, not a magic answer. The profitable exploit depends on how opponents respond. These scenarios teach concepts; they do not claim solver precision.
